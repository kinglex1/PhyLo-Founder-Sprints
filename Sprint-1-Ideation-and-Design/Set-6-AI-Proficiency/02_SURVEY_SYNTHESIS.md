## Key Insights

### Strongest Signals

1. **Pain is real and quantified:** Respondents are tracking their losses (6-10%) and wasting time (avg 7.75 hrs/week)

2. **Trust deficit exists:** Current systems score only 5/10 for trust - opportunity for better solution

3. **Root cause identification is the #1 ask:** 50% want to know WHY fuel was lost, not just WHEN

4. **Financial pressure is mounting:** Rising fuel costs amplify the impact of losses

5. **Audit-grade reports have high value:** 62.5% rate at 7+/10

---

## Recommendations

### Product Priorities

1. **Root cause classification** - Distinguish theft from operational issues
2. **Audit-grade documentation** - Reports that can be used for action
3. **Real-time monitoring** - Address "delayed alerts" complaint
4. **Low false positive rate** - Build trust through accuracy

### Go-to-Market

1. **Lead with ROI** - Quantify savings (7.75 hrs/week = money saved)
2. **Emphasize proof** - "Audit-grade" resonates
3. **Subscription model** - Aligns with 62.5% preference
4. **Contact warm leads** - 4 interested parties to follow up
