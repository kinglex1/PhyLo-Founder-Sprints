## Key Findings Summary

### Pain Points Identified

1. **Fuel Loss Percentage:** Most respondents (75%) estimate 6-10% monthly loss
2. **Trust in Current Systems:** Low (avg 5/10) - systems not reliable
3. **Root Cause Identification:** Biggest frustration - can't distinguish theft from operational issues
4. **Lack of Proof:** Systems alert but don't provide evidence
5. **Financial Impact:** Losses cut into thin margins, affect profitability
6. **Time Waste:** Average 7.5 hours/week investigating false alarms

### Value Proposition Validation

- **Audit-Grade Reports:** 8/8 respondents rated value 5-9/10 (avg 7.1)
- **Pricing Preference:** 5/8 prefer fixed monthly subscription
- **Contact Interest:** 4/8 want follow-up discussions
