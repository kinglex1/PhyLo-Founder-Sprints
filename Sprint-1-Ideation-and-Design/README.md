## Sprint Outcomes (What We Decided)

**Selected business:** Month-End Fuel Audit
**Selected buyer:** Fleet Operations Manager (champion) + Finance Director (payer)
**Selected route profile:** Cross-border fleets, specifically Rustenburg → Harare
**Selected pricing model:** Per-fleet monthly subscription, R8,000–R15,000/fleet/month, with freemium tier
**Selected technical wedge:** Physics-informed engine that sits on top of existing telematics, validates output, filters false alerts, and produces audit-grade reports
