How to read these three options

PhyLo does not replace your Cartrack, Tracker, Netstar, Webfleet, or MiX subscription. PhyLo reads the telematics feed you already have and produces the month-end audit document those dashboards cannot. Choose which way you'd prefer to pay for that layer.

Path A — Default for typical customers
Per-vehicle subscription
R45/vehicle/month
Tiered: R35 (100-200), R25 (>200)
  • Dashboard, monthly report, alerts
  • 1 dispute pack per quarter
  • Reads your existing telematics feed
  • ~20% of your Cartrack basic subscription
Less than a fifth of your Cartrack bill. Sits on top, not in place of.
Path B — Default for risk-averse customers
Per-incident only
R2,000/confirmed event
No monthly floor
  • Detection engine, evidence pack, monthly report
  • Pay nothing monthly — R2,000 per proven event
  • Events above 85% confidence only
  • No per-vehicle number to compare to Cartrack
Pay nothing monthly. R2,000 per theft event PhyLo proves.
Path C — Default for high-variance (pilot only)
Soft floor + per-incident
R1,500/month floor
+ R1,500 per confirmed theft
  • Floor covers up to 200 vehicles
  • R1,500 per event PhyLo proves
  • Cheapest defensible vs. Cartrack+PhyLo combined bill
  • Best for high-variance customers (R50k+/month)
R1,500 floor + R1,500 per proven event. ~20% of recovered value.

All three paths start with a 30-day free diagnostic. PhyLo reads your telematics feed, produces a baseline loss report. You see your actual loss number in writing before you pick a path. The diagnostic is the sale.

Why these prices: PhyLo Path A (R45/vehicle) is ~20% of Cartrack basic (R200-250/vehicle). A 60-truck fleet pays R13,200 Cartrack + R2,700 PhyLo Path A = R15,900 combined, a 20% premium on Cartrack alone (actual uplift R2,700 / R13,200 = 20.45%, rounded). The layer-not-replacement framing survives this bill. PhyLo at R150/vehicle (an earlier draft) would have been 60-75% above Cartrack — indefensible as a "layer."
