Part 4 — Human vs Agent Decision Framework

For every M6+ role, ask:
  1. One-off, low-volume, relationship-driven? → Human (pilot account mgmt, fundraising, key hires)
  2. Repeatable, high-volume, rules-based? → Agent (transcript synthesis, draft generation, monitoring)
  3. Creative + quality-sensitive + needs founder voice? → Combined (marketing, dispute packs, SOWs)
  4. Regulatory / legal / financial? → Human + compliance (bookkeeper, attorney, tax)
