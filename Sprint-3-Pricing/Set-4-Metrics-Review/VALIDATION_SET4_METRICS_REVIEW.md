## Part 1: Slide-by-Slide Outline

The deck is at [`METRICS_REVIEW_DECK.html`](./METRICS_REVIEW_DECK.html) — open in a browser, press arrow keys or use the on-screen controls. 5 slides, each <50 words, each with one chart or visual.

### Slide 1 — Selected Business + Buyer
- **Title:** "PhyLo: The Month-End Fuel Audit"
- **Visual:** Buyer persona composite (Kgabo Moloto, Fleet Ops Manager + Lerato Ndlovu, Finance Director)
- **Script (~45 words):** "PhyLo is the month-end fuel audit. Kgabo, fleet operations manager, runs 60 trucks. R12-18M a year on diesel. Lerato, finance director, asks every month: where did the variance go? PhyLo gives Kgabo the document to hand Lerato."

### Slide 2 — The Pain (with anchors)
- **Title:** "R78,000/day stolen. R3.6B/year nationally. Cartrack cannot see it."
- **Visual:** SA fuel-fraud case-study table from the research report (Avis R3M / 8 months, eThekwini R78k/day, Rajasthan R400k/year)
- **Script (~45 words):** "R78,000 a day stolen from a single municipal fleet. R3.6 billion a year nationally. Cartrack, Tracker, and Netstar can flag the anomaly. None of them can produce the document. PhyLo reads the same telematics feed and turns it into the proof."

### Slide 3 — Revenue Model + Pricing
- **Title:** "R45/vehicle OR R1,500 floor + R1,500/event. Diagnostic-led."
- **Visual:** Comparison table (telematics subscription vs. PhyLo Path A or Path C) + combined-bill defensibility check
- **Script (~48 words):** "PhyLo's three pricing paths after a free 30-day diagnostic. Path A: R45 a vehicle — 20% of Cartrack basic. Path B: R2,000 per event, no floor. Path C: R1,500 a month plus R1,500 per proven event. Cartrack plus PhyLo combined: 20% above Cartrack alone. The layer framing survives the bill."

### Slide 4 — Unit Economics
- **Title:** "Customer ROI 22x. Payback 4.4 months. R1M ARR = 31 customers (Path A) or 8 (Path C)."
- **Visual:** LTV / CAC / payback triangle + customer-count comparison
- **Script (~45 words):** "Path A: LTV R29,160 at 30% margin (honest, sub-bar). The customer ROI is 22x: R720k+ per year recovered, R32k paid. Payback 4.4 months. R1M ARR: 31 customers Path A, 8 high-variance Path C, or 7 Path B (no floor). The diagnostic conversion rate is the constraint, not the price."

### Slide 5 — Team + Ask
- **Title:** "Solo founder + AI agent → R69,400/6mo run cost → R1M ARR = 31 Path A or 8 Path C."
- **Visual:** 6-month cost table (R69,400) + customer-count scenario
- **Script (~45 words):** "Six-month run cost: R69,400. R50k is the first sales hire at month five. R4,400 is agent API fees. R15k is tool subscriptions. The ask: 31 Path A customers by month six, or 8 Path C, or 16 plus 4 mixed. Diagnostic conversion is the constraint, not the price."

**Total word count of all 5 scripts: ~220 words = ~90 seconds at 145 wpm + visual transitions = 2:45-3:00. Under 3 minutes.**
