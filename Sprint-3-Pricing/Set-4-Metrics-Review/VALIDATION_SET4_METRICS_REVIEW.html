Slide 1 — Selected Business + Buyer
PhyLo: The Month-End Fuel Audit

Visual: Buyer persona composite (Kgabo Moloto, Fleet Ops Manager + Lerato Ndlovu, Finance Director).

"PhyLo is the month-end fuel audit. Kgabo, fleet operations manager, runs 60 trucks. R12-18M a year on diesel. Lerato, finance director, asks every month: where did the variance go? PhyLo gives Kgabo the document to hand Lerato."

Slide 2 — The Pain (with anchors)
R78,000/day stolen. R3.6B/year nationally. Cartrack cannot see it.

Visual: SA fuel-fraud case-study table (eThekwini R78k/day, national R3.6B/yr, Avis R3M/8mo, Rajasthan R400k/yr).

"R78,000 a day stolen from a single municipal fleet. R3.6 billion a year nationally. Cartrack, Tracker, and Netstar can flag the anomaly. None of them can produce the document. PhyLo reads the same telematics feed and turns it into the proof."
~45 words · 18 sec at 145 wpm
Slide 3 — Revenue Model + Pricing
R45/vehicle OR R1,500 floor + R1,500/event. Diagnostic-led.

Visual: Comparison table (telematics vs PhyLo Path A or Path C) + combined-bill defensibility check.

"PhyLo's three pricing paths after a free 30-day diagnostic. Path A: R45 a vehicle — 20% of Cartrack basic. Path B: R2,000 per event, no floor. Path C: R1,500 a month plus R1,500 per proven event. Cartrack plus PhyLo combined: 20% above Cartrack alone. The layer framing survives the bill."
~45 words · 18 sec at 145 wpm
Slide 4 — Unit Economics
Customer ROI 22x. Payback 4.4 months. R1M ARR = 31 customers (Path A), 8 (Path C), 7 (Path B).

Visual: LTV / CAC / payback KPI row + customer-count triangle (31 Path A / 8 Path C / 7 Path B).

"Path A: LTV R29,160 at 30% margin (honest, sub-bar). The customer ROI is 22x: R720k+ per year recovered, R32k paid. Payback 4.4 months. R1M ARR: 31 customers Path A, 8 high-variance Path C, or 7 Path B (no floor). The diagnostic conversion rate is the constraint, not the price."
~40 words · 17 sec at 145 wpm
Slide 5 — Team + Ask
Solo founder + AI agent → R69,400/6mo run cost → R1M ARR = 31 Path A or 8 Path C.

Visual: 6-month cost table (R50k human + R4.4k agent + R15k subs = R69.4k total).

"Six-month run cost: R69,400. R50k is the first sales hire at month five. R4,400 is agent API fees. R15k is tool subscriptions. The ask: 31 Path A customers by month six, or 8 Path C, or 16 plus 4 mixed. Diagnostic conversion is the constraint, not the price."
~45 words · 18 sec at 145 wpm
