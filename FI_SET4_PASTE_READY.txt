# Part 2: LLM Feedback and Refinements

After running my four sentences through an LLM for unfiltered feedback, the main improvements were:

1. Sentence 2 was too long and overstuffed — dropped "OmniTeknology" and the AI tools reference. Kept the 30% fuel theft hook as the centerpiece.
2. Sentence 3 had too much qualifying language ("I don't know how to," "I need to"). Reframed to lead with what mentors bring to the table.
3. All hedging removed — no "trying to," no "I think," no "maybe." Direct statements only.
