## The Pitch (Verbatim)

### Hook (0:00–0:20)
> "Last quarter a 42-vehicle fleet running Rustenburg to Harare lost R78,000 of diesel they couldn't explain. The same number they lose every month. They have GPS, sensors, telematics, fuel cards. They get the alerts. They just can't prove what happened."

### Problem (0:20–0:55)
> "Commercial fleets in South Africa lose 5 to 8 percent of fuel spend to unexplained variance every month. R1.5 billion a year industry-wide. The problem isn't detection — it's accountability. Existing systems generate false-positive alerts that ops teams stop trusting. They generate dashboards no one reads. Flags no one acts on. So the ops manager shows up to the monthly finance meeting with no document, no explanation, and no way to act on the theft they know is happening."

### Solution (0:55–1:40)
> "PhyLo is the audit-grade fuel monitor for South African fleets. Our physics-informed engine eliminates false positives from dual-tank fuel shift. It attributes cross-border theft at borders like Plumtree and Beitbridge. And at month-end, it produces the document no other system does: a vehicle-by-vehicle audit report. Confidence scores, alternative hypotheses eliminated, loss values in Rands, chain of custody verified. This is the document ops managers hand to finance. The proof they need for CCMA hearings. The case file for SAPS or insurance."

### Business Model + Traction (1:40–2:25)
> "We sell per-fleet monthly subscriptions at R8,000 to R15,000 per fleet per month. Premium tier for legal-grade case file reports. We use freemium: a shareable one-page audit report becomes our acquisition engine — ops managers forward to finance directors, finance directors see the value, we close. TAM is R1.35 billion in South Africa. Path to R1 million ARR is 8 to 10 fleet customers in year one. We have a pilot running today with the Rustenburg-Harare fleet. Pilot partner confirmed the pain. Working group and mentor feedback validated the wedge. We're building."

### Moat + Ask (2:25–3:00)
> "Our moat is workflow integration, not features. Once PhyLo becomes the document finance relies on, the switching cost is high. The audit-grade format, the physics-based confidence scoring — dashboard-only competitors cannot match this. What I'm looking for: intros to fleet operators managing 50-plus vehicles. Partnership conversations with telematics providers and fuel card networks. Both of these are existing ecosystems with customer trust already built — exactly the leverage model Kaylash described. If you know any of these, let's talk after this."
