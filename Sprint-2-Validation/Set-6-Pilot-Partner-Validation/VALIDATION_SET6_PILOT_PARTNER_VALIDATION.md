## Part 4: One-Sentence Pitch Tailored to Pilot Partner

The pitch is written in the pilot partner's own language — drawn from the Sprint 1 pilot partner conversation and the Set 5 audit report format.

### Draft Pitch (Pre-Call)

> "PhyLo gives you the document you need to tell finance where every litre went — and the proof you need to actually do something about the theft you're already paying for."

### Why This Pitch

- **"the document you need to tell finance"** — directly addresses the month-end reconciliation pain ("Finance wants a number. The number keeps being 5–8% higher than it should be.")
- **"the proof you need to actually do something"** — directly addresses the "nothing changes" pain ("We have sensors. We have telematics. Nothing changes.")
- **"the theft you're already paying for"** — frames the cost as a current expense, not a future risk. The operator is already losing R78,000/month; PhyLo is the recovery, not a new spend.

## Part 5: Pilot Scope Proposal (To Share at End of Call)

### 30-Day Pilot — Proposed Structure

**What PhyLo provides (free for 30 days):**
- Read-only access to telematics data for 5 of the pilot partner's vehicles
- Physics-informed monitoring: dual-tank false positive elimination, cross-border edge case handling
- Real-time alert filtering (only validated alerts reach the pilot partner)
- One full month-end audit report at the end of the pilot
- Side-by-side comparison: what your current system flagged vs what PhyLo validated

**What the pilot partner provides:**
- Telematics data access (read-only API or CSV export)
- One hour per week for a feedback call
- Permission to use the pilot results (anonymized) as a case study

**What happens after the pilot:**
- If validated: PhyLo extends to the full fleet (42 vehicles) at the agreed pricing
- If not validated: pilot partner keeps the report, no obligation, relationship continues

**Estimated pilot value to the pilot partner:**
- 5 vehicles × R1,500/vehicle/year × 30 days = R62.50 of value (mathematically)
- Real value: validation of the system, audit report, false positive noise eliminated for 30 days
- Intangible value: PhyLo team learns the pilot partner's specific routes, edge cases, and operational context
